
# FISHING Game

## Loop

##### Catching Phase 
- You need to catch 5 fish per round
- Get X baits or whatever, can release fish to swim agian but cannot be caught agian this round because they learnede their wayhs or whatevber
- There are X number of fish in your hatchery
- Start each run with one basic fish species in the pond; buy other species during the run
- Each round you can minipulate the pond by breeding, feeding, or killing fish 
    - These can be bought in the store inbetween rounds, no acces the first round
- Caught fish are removed from the pond
    - Forces future purchases of fish to breed
- If pond does not have enough fish, this phase is skipped and someone offers to sell you enough fish to put back in the pond
    - These fish are marked up and are babys, cost goes agianst when you sell the fish
    - Either a percent amount or like a strict dollar amount

##### Intermediate phase
- Once 5 fish are caught, you can chose to refine the fish or sell them
    - Refine to fufull orders:
        - Cook / Slice fish to make them more valuable
        - Sell to restruants and more lucrative locations and fufill their orders
    - Sell raw - Multiple fish of the same kind can earn a bonus through a signed contract
        - Sell to fish merchants/reselers
        - Lower sell price but no infastructure requries
        - Without a relevant contract, matching fish only earn their normal sale value
- If there is insufucent funds, can take out a loan
    - Rates determined by "credit score"
        - Based on how offten you make payments back
        - The more times you make payments, the lower your future intrest rates will be
- Can purchase chemicals, food, infastructure, and new fish types to improve pond
    - Shop offers are randomized between rounds, giving each run different build opportunities
    - Buy fish packs, chemical packs, food packs, and other packs; machines appear occasionally
    - Choose purchases that support your contracts and the species you want in the pond

##### Payback Phase
- Chose to make payments on debt
    - Start with $X in debt because you bought your parents old fish hatchery or smth
        - Dont have to pay off debt each round
        - Enough subsuquent failures to pay off debt will result in assets being seized
        - Eventual bankruptcy
    - Can chose to pay intrest or core balence depending on loan type
    - Can invest money for the future

##### Between-round pond changes
- Fish remaining in the pond have a chance to breed after each round
- Released fish become available to catch again next round
- Selling removes stock, so keeping breeding fish and restocking are part of maintaining a build

## Game Objects

##### Fish
- Start with X Number in pond, all of one basic species
- Can purchase new types at various ages
    - New species are introduced through fish packs rather than all being present at the start
- Each round, fish mature 
    - More mature fish score more points
- When caught, fish are removed from the pond
    - Will need to restock pond in the future
- Can be bread with breed specific food or killed with specific pesticides
- Have a chance to naturally breed after each round
    - Species-specific food can encourage breeding
    - Keeping breeding stock competes with selling fish for immediate income
- Crossbreeding? 
    - Physicaly possible between closely relatede species
- Fish can have mutations that make them more valuable
    - Diffrent metals (gold, silver, bronze)
    - Think of more...
- Can be unhealthy --> put medicin in the pond or something


##### Shop / Packs
- Balatro-inspired shop: a limited selection of random offers each shop visit
    - Offers help the player build a pond around contracts and catch combinations
    - Work toward cleansing the pond of unwanted fish to improve the odds of useful catches
- Fish packs
    - Contain random fish with chances of specific rarities
    - Buy new species or add more of a species already in the pond
    - More valuable species can be unlocked across multiple playthroughs
    - Unlocked species become eligible for future fish packs
- Chemical packs
    - Pond management, medicine, selective fish removal, and water treatments
- Food packs
    - Growth food and food that encourages breeding for particular species
- Other pack types can be added as the game's systems develop
- Machines appear occasionally as individual offers
    - Permanent equipment can support a particular pond build
- Exact pack contents, rarity odds, prices, and offer frequency still need balancing


##### Contracts / Catch Combos
- Sign a relevant contract to receive a multiple-of-a-kind bonus
    - Matching species do not automatically receive a combo bonus without a contract
    - The bonus is added to normal fish sale value when the contract's requirements are met
- Contracts give the player a reason to stock, breed, or remove particular fish
- Contract requirements should be visible while fishing
- Example contract ideas, with thresholds and payouts still to be balanced:
    - Carp wholesaler: bonus for selling three or more carp in one round
    - Tuna restaurant: bonus for selling two tuna in one round
    - Bulk supplier: bonus for selling five fish of the same species in one round
- Contract duration, acquisition, slot limits, and whether bonuses can stack are still open decisions


##### Ideas to test for the shop and breeding
- Offer contracts early so players have a direction when choosing packs
- Packs could reveal several choices and let the player keep one or two
    - Show rarity odds before purchase
- A paid shop reroll could give players another use for their earnings
- Keep a basic restocking option available when random offers do not help
- Breeding could roll once per pair of the same species, influenced by food
    - A pond-capacity limit could prevent unchecked population growth
    - Exact breeding timing relative to purchases and food application is still to be decided
- Fish unlocks could come from milestones such as completing runs, breeding species, or fulfilling contracts
    - Valuable unlocked species can have costs or tradeoffs that create different builds
- First prototype could focus on a few species, contracts, breeding, cleansing, and a small random shop

##### Loans/Debt
- Player starts in debt from buying the property
- This debt can be payed back at any time scale
- Can take on other loans throughout to finance things
    - Short term
        - Shorter payback period, lower intrest, lower amount to borrow, higher payments
    - Long term
        - Longer payback, higher intrest, most money avaliable to borrow, lower payments
        - Can pay just intrest
    - Family can come offer you money
        - Lowest intrest rate, less presure to payback, no choice in amount borrowed, rare but a small one is offerd to players after the first round
- Loans taken on during gameplay need to be paid off to win
    - For all loans besides family and the property, you need to make __A__ payment each round
- Amount of money avaliable and inntrest rate is based on credit score
- Defualting
    - Long and short term:
        - Reduces credit score
        - After X number of defualts, assets can be seized
    - Family:
        - Can defualt a couple times
        - Leads to family member wanting to have some % of profits until loan is repayed
- Specific items can have 0% financing (machines, get lucky with a sale)
