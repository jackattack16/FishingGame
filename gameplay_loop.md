
# FISHING Game

## Loop

##### Catching Phase 
- You need to catch 5 fish per round
- Get X baits or whatever, can release fish to swim agian but cannot be caught agian this round because they learnede their wayhs or whatevber
- There are X number of fish in your hatchery
- Each round you can minipulate the pond by breeding, feeding, or killing fish 
    - These can be bought in the store inbetween rounds, no acces the first round
- Caught fish are removed from the pond
    - Forces future purchases of fish to breed
- If pond does not have enough fish, this phase is skipped and someone offers to sell you enough fish to put back in the pond
    - These fish are marked up and are babys, cost goes agianst when you sell the fish
    - Either a percent amount or like a strict dollar amount

##### Intermediate phase
- Once 5 fish are caught, you can chose to refine the fish or sell them
    - Refine to fufull orders:
        - Cook / Slice fish to make them more valuable
        - Sell to restruants and more lucrative locations and fufill their orders
    - Sell raw - More of one kind can increse sell value, economy of scale or whatever 
        - Sell to fish merchants/reselers
        - Lower sell price but no infastructure requries
- If there is insufucent funds, can take out a loan
    - Rates determined by "credit score"
        - Based on how offten you make payments back
        - The more times you make payments, the lower your future intrest rates will be
- Can purchase chemicals, food, infastructure, and new fish types to improve pond

##### Payback Phase
- Chose to make payments on debt
    - Start with $X in debt because you bought your parents old fish hatchery or smth
        - Dont have to pay off debt each round
        - Enough subsuquent failures to pay off debt will result in assets being seized
        - Eventual bankruptcy
    - Can chose to pay intrest or core balence depending on loan type
    - Can invest money for the future

##### Loans/Debt
- Player starts in debt from buying the property
- This debt can be payed back at any time scale
- Can take on other loans throughout to finance things
    - Short term
        - Shorter payback period, lower intrest, lower amount to borrow, higher payments
    - Long term
        - Longer payback, higher intrest, most money avaliable to borrow, lower payments
        - Can pay just intrest
    - Family can come offer you money
        - Lowest intrest rate, less presure to payback, no choice in amount borrowed, rare but a small one is offerd to players after the first round
- Loans taken on during gameplay need to be paid off to win
    - For all loans besides family and the property, you need to make __A__ payment each round
- Amount of money avaliable and inntrest rate is based on credit score
- Defualting
    - Long and short term:
        - Reduces credit score
        - After X number of defualts, assets can be seized
    - Family:
        - Can defualt a couple times
        - Leads to family member wanting to have some % of profits until loan is repayed
- Specific items can have 0% financing (machines, get lucky with a sale)
